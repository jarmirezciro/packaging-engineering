Calculator outputs

Understand capacity, usage, and weight

The result separates geometric bag fit from optional weight and payload checks so each restriction stays visible.

Quantity and remaining capacity

Maximum quantity is the largest supported arrangement that fits. Remaining capacity compares that maximum with the current quantity.

Current and maximum bag usage

Usage compares required flat area with selected bag area for the current quantity and for the calculated maximum quantity.

Weight and payload

Product weight drives net weight. Packaging tare adds total weight, while maximum payload enables payload usage.

How to use it

Three steps to evaluate a bag

1

Measure the product

Use the rectangular external length, width, and height of the product or grouped base unit.

2

Enter the flat bag

Use physical bag length and width. Add product weight, bag tare, and maximum payload when available.

3

Run and review

Check fit, quantity, usage, weights, payload, orientation graphic and assumptions. Downloadable PDF reports are included with KolliPack Plus.

Engineering restrictions

What the result assumes

The calculator models rectangular bounding geometry and supported deterministic arrangements. It does not infer film stretch, gussets, valves, compression, or irregular product deformation.

Length, sealing, and tolerance

Bag length includes the reserved sealing allowance and tolerance. Bag width is the opening direction and receives tolerance only.

Payload is a separate constraint

A geometric fit can still be unsuitable if net product weight exceeds the real bag's payload, seal strength, or handling limit.
