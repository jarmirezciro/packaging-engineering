The challenge

The decisions that shape logistics start earlier than transport.

Product dimensions, packaging choices and pallet patterns influence material use, handling, transport capacity and cost. Yet these choices are often evaluated separately.

Packaging affects the whole load

Package dimensions and pallet patterns influence how efficiently products use pallets, vehicles and containers.

Technical choices are hard to compare

A change to the product or package can affect palletization, transport loading and the overall logistics flow.

Expertise is difficult to reuse

Clear tools, visual results and common methods make engineering and operational knowledge easier to share and repeat.
