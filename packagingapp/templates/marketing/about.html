Why we exist

Packaging is often underestimated, but small packaging decisions can create large operational impact.

Many companies have strong product, purchasing, logistics, and quality processes, but packaging decisions are still handled with scattered spreadsheets, supplier catalogues, experience, and manual trial-and-error.

Savings potential

Better package dimensions, pallet patterns, and transport loading can reduce waste, air, handling, damage, and freight inefficiency.

Disconnected decisions

Product, carton, pallet, and transport choices are often optimized separately even though they strongly affect each other.

Training gap

There are not many formal, practical packaging engineering education paths, especially for teams that need applied industrial methods.
