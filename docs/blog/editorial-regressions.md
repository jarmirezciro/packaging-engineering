# KolliLabs editorial regressions

Use this register during article review with the [editorial examples](editorial-examples.md). It gives repeatable checks for recurring problems under the existing production standards. Checks marked **deterministic** use inspectable data, markup or file identity. **Visual** checks require viewing the asset or page. **Editorial** checks require a judgment about meaning, accuracy, audience or scope.

| ID | Failure condition | Expected behavior | Examples and applicable profiles | How to verify |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| EDR-01 | The lead assumes an unfamiliar SKU, fixture or software setting, or leaves the packaging decision buried in background. | Name the physical problem and reader decision early. In a demo, give the verified outcome within the existing opening requirements. | [TOPS-04](editorial-examples.md#003-tops-maxload-benchmark), [ORI-01](editorial-examples.md#006-product-orientation-demo). All three profiles. | **Editorial.** Read the opening by itself. Identify the subject, what changed, and what the result answers. |
| EDR-02 | Reader-facing copy contains reviewer instructions, drafting commentary, vague product language, or the same defensive caveat at multiple points. | Address the intended reader in precise packaging terms. Keep source follow-up in the evidence record. Include attribution and a concise disclosure of settings and limits that affect interpretation. | [CAPE-08 and CAPE-09](editorial-examples.md#002-capepack-bottle-benchmark), [BOX-01](editorial-examples.md#001-box-selection). All three profiles. | **Editorial.** Read the caption, opening, source note and conclusion in sequence. Check that uncertainties are disclosed where they matter and internal actions stay out of the article. |
| EDR-03 | A fixture, method, result or operational consequence is introduced too late, repeated without purpose, or mixed with a different type of comparison. | Put related setup details together. Explain methods before interpreting them, and separate academic and vendor results when they support different claims. Keep single-decision demos short and provide the detail needed for a deep dive or benchmark. | [CAPE-03](editorial-examples.md#002-capepack-bottle-benchmark), [TOPS-05](editorial-examples.md#003-tops-maxload-benchmark), [BR-03 and BR-04](editorial-examples.md#005-br1br7-container-benchmark), [ORI-01 and ORI-02](editorial-examples.md#006-product-orientation-demo). All three profiles. | **Editorial.** Read the heading sequence with the opening and conclusion. Follow each claim from the setup and method to its evidence and practical consequence. |
| EDR-04 | Geometry, labels, construction cues or visible counts misstate or obscure the engineering principle. | Use verified geometry and numbers when the figure makes an exact claim. Show the construction detail needed to identify the object or mechanism. Keep calculations intact when improving their graphic treatment. | [BOX-04 and BOX-05](editorial-examples.md#001-box-selection), [RSC-01 through RSC-03](editorial-examples.md#004-rsc-material-optimization). All three profiles when engineering details are depicted. | **Deterministic.** Compare explicit numbers, dimensions and units with sources. **Visual and editorial.** Inspect flaps, seams, planes, orientations or geometry against the source and the figure's stated purpose. |
| EDR-05 | A necessary physical setup is missing, starts with UI alone, or includes stages unsupported by the case. | Introduce the actual product and packaging relationship before software screens where the physical context helps. Include only in-scope stages and verified constraints. Identify conceptual artwork as such. | [CAPE-01](editorial-examples.md#002-capepack-bottle-benchmark), [TOPS-04](editorial-examples.md#003-tops-maxload-benchmark), [ORI-03](editorial-examples.md#006-product-orientation-demo). All three profiles when setup needs illustration. | **Visual and editorial.** Compare page order, pictured objects and labeled inputs with the brief and run record. Apply the production [Packaging Scenario Setup](../../.agents/skills/blog-article/references/packaging-scenario-setup.md). |
| EDR-06 | Blank framing, interface chrome or an embedded table makes a comparator panel or load geometry too small to interpret. | Compose faithful source crops around one question. Keep the actual rendering legible when geometry is the focus and move detailed numbers into a separate table when it helps. Preserve the original capture and its source labels. | [CAPE-02, CAPE-04 and CAPE-05](editorial-examples.md#002-capepack-bottle-benchmark), [TOPS-01 and TOPS-03](editorial-examples.md#003-tops-maxload-benchmark). Comparative Benchmark and Practical Demo, plus Deep Dive when using source captures. | **Visual.** Compare native and page-scale figures with original captures and check labels and the focal point at mobile width. Use the existing [comparative figure workflow](../../.agents/skills/blog-article/references/comparative-figure-workflow.md) and its separate layout for tables used as evidence. |
| EDR-07 | A results table leaks pipe syntax, loses header and unit context, or appears without the established treatment. | Deliver a semantic rendered table with clear headers, units and readable comparisons. Use the production blog results-table pattern where applicable. | [BOX-03](editorial-examples.md#001-box-selection), [CAPE-06](editorial-examples.md#002-capepack-bottle-benchmark), [TOPS-02](editorial-examples.md#003-tops-maxload-benchmark), [RSC-04](editorial-examples.md#004-rsc-material-optimization). All three profiles. | **Deterministic.** Inspect rendered HTML for a table, headers, expected values and leaked Markdown. **Visual.** Compare the actual desktop and narrow-page result with the approved treatment. |
| EDR-08 | Figure numbering is absent or inconsistent between article order, caption, text references and printed artwork. | Use a coherent sequential figure series when referring to figures in the article. Keep the caption accurate and agree with any number in the image. | [BOX-06](editorial-examples.md#001-box-selection), [ORI-04](editorial-examples.md#006-product-orientation-demo). All three profiles when figures are referenced or presented as a series. | **Deterministic.** Compare caption order and references in source. **Visual.** Compare with numbers printed in the actual asset. **Editorial.** Read captions for purpose and scope. |
| EDR-09 | A thumbnail candidate diverges from the approved studio family, loses its hook at card size, or inherits approval from a different file. | Follow the canonical board and actual thumbnail workflow. Keep exact claims tied to evidence and approval tied to the candidate bytes. | [CAPE-07](editorial-examples.md#002-capepack-bottle-benchmark), [BR-01 and BR-02](editorial-examples.md#005-br1br7-container-benchmark). All three profiles. | **Deterministic.** Check file, dimensions, candidate identity and approval record. **Visual.** Compare the card at normal size and 360 × 203 with the canonical board. |
| EDR-10 | A source value changes without a recorded derivation, a crop hides a relevant condition, or an academic or vendor result is presented as a KolliPack calculation. | Identify each result's source, date, fixture and constraints. Verify metrics and conversions, preserve comparable conditions, and disclose unresolved differences. Scope conclusions to the evidence. | [CAPE-08](editorial-examples.md#002-capepack-bottle-benchmark), [TOPS-01 and TOPS-05](editorial-examples.md#003-tops-maxload-benchmark), [BR-04](editorial-examples.md#005-br1br7-container-benchmark). All three profiles with sourced claims. | **Deterministic.** Trace figures and claims to a source or run ID and reconcile numbers and units. **Visual.** Compare crops and displayed data with originals. **Editorial.** Verify the claim's attribution and limitations using the [archive contract](evidence-archive-contract.md). |
| EDR-11 | A runnable article directs readers to an empty calculator or loads the wrong scenario. | Use a named prepopulated case for articles subject to the demo policy. Align the CTA and body links with the intended fixture and explain which inputs open and what can be changed. | [CAPE-10](editorial-examples.md#002-capepack-bottle-benchmark), [TOPS case link](editorial-examples.md#003-tops-maxload-benchmark), [ORI-01](editorial-examples.md#006-product-orientation-demo). Practical Demo and Comparative Benchmark where runnable or published as `demo_case_study`. | **Deterministic.** Check the route, case key and preset mapping, then verify prefilled inputs in future article QA. **Editorial.** Check that the link's promised action matches the loaded scenario. |
| EDR-12 | Approved copy or artwork is silently rewritten, replaced or removed while addressing another issue. | Track approval for the exact element and version. Preserve a recoverable copy of the approved artifact and follow the preservation mode when integrating owner-approved work. | [BOX-04 and BOX-05](editorial-examples.md#001-box-selection), [CAPE-05](editorial-examples.md#002-capepack-bottle-benchmark), [BR-01 and BR-02](editorial-examples.md#005-br1br7-container-benchmark). All three profiles. | **Deterministic.** Compare text, references and asset hashes to the approved record. **Editorial.** Confirm the change scope against the decision recorded under [publish approved](../../.agents/skills/blog-article/references/publish-approved-artifact.md). |

A green accent in the TOPS figures, additional insight boxes, a specific four-panel CapePack replacement, and a new application example for RSC are editorial options. Choose them when they improve the intended article and have appropriate source support. Use the existing [Visual Editorial Critic](../../.agents/skills/blog-article/references/visual-editorial-critic.md) for judgments about composition, legibility and fit.

## Production guidance that needs clarification

| Finding | Production evidence | Interpretation for this register |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Older thumbnail instructions coexist with the current standard | The [graphic style guide](../../.agents/skills/blog-article/references/kollipack-graphic-style-guide.md) still describes a screenshot or render plus top-left logo in its thumbnail sections. Its own scope note and the [thumbnail workflow](../../.agents/skills/blog-article/references/thumbnail-workflow.md) assign new thumbnails to the dark studio system, which uses a lower-left lockup. | Use the explicit thumbnail-specific standard for new candidates. Retain the light system for article figures. |
| BR1–BR7 thumbnail has approvals with different scopes | The [design-system implementation record](thumbnail-design-system-v1.md#repository-implementation-record-2026-10-20) names the approved v1 pilot. The owner explicitly approved the visible v2 selected by the published article. | Cite [BR-02](editorial-examples.md#005-br1br7-container-benchmark) for the published appearance. Cite the implementation record for the file-specific v1 approval. |
| The result-first checklist has broader wording than the editorial style | The [quality checklist](../../.agents/skills/blog-article/references/quality-checklist.md) gives a first-100-words item without a profile qualifier. The [editorial style](../../.agents/skills/blog-article/references/editorial-style.md) scopes it to demo and business articles. | Judge whether the opening explains the actual decision in each profile. The existing demo requirement still applies to demos. |
| Skill profiles and metadata do different jobs | The [main skill](../../.agents/skills/blog-article/SKILL.md) includes `business_insight`; the [article schema](../../.agents/skills/blog-article/references/article-schema.md#approved-article-types) omits it. The registry uses both `demo_case_study` and `engineering_deep_dive` for comparative benchmarks. Published CapePack also uses a generic CTA despite the [demo profile's case requirement](../../.agents/skills/blog-article/references/demo-case-study-profile.md#prepopulated-case-requirement). | Use the three calibration profiles to choose examples. Preserve the existing metadata schema and check applicable demo requirements. |
