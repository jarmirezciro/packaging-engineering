# KolliLabs company context

## Identity

- **Company:** KolliLabs

- **Primary domain direction:** `kollilabs.com`
- **Business type:** independent commercial packaging-engineering software, consultancy, and education initiative.

Do not describe KolliPack as a Volvo product or internal corporate system.

## Why KolliLabs exists

Packaging is often understudied inside companies even though it can materially influence:

- material cost;
- product damage and quality;
- warehouse and handling efficiency;
- pallet and transport utilization;
- logistics cost;
- sustainability and carbon impact;
- customer experience and operational standardization.

Formal and practical packaging education is also limited compared with adjacent disciplines. KolliLabs exists to make packaging engineering more accessible through software, practical knowledge, consultancy, and education.

## Offer structure

KolliLabs can present three connected offers:

1. **Software** — KolliPack and future packaging applications.
2. **Packaging consultancy** — practical analysis, optimization, and implementation support.
3. **Packaging education** — articles, guides, training, and explanatory tools.

Future software concepts discussed include artwork management and AI-assisted flexible-packaging visualization. These are roadmap ideas, not necessarily shipped capabilities.

## Voice

Public copy should be:

- technically credible but understandable;
- practical and benefit-oriented;
- European/Nordic in visual restraint;
- honest about assumptions;
- focused on measurable packaging and logistics decisions.

Avoid inflated claims, vague “AI-powered” language, and unsupported savings promises.
