# Public KolliPack knowledge

## KolliLabs and KolliPack

KolliLabs is an independent packaging-engineering software, consultancy, and education company. KolliPack is its packaging-engineering application. KolliPack connects deterministic tools for package selection, palletization, transport loading, shared catalogues, visual explanation, reports, and an integrated Packaging Flow.

## Available KolliPack tools

Current KolliPack tool families include Product and Packaging Catalogues, Container Selection, Multi-product Container Selection, Bag Selection, Multi-product Bag Selection, Palletization, Transport Container Loading, Corrugated Material & Strength, and Packaging Flow. Public no-account calculators are available for Box Size, Bag Size, Palletization, and Container Loading. Some application capabilities, catalogues, and reports depend on account or plan access.

## Choosing a tool

- Use Container Selection to estimate how rectangular products fit in a box or container, or to compare suitable box candidates.
- Use Bag Selection to evaluate lay-flat bag dimensions with fit tolerance and sealing margin.
- Use Palletization to arrange cartons on a pallet and evaluate layers, quantity, height, overhang, and entered weight limits.
- Use Transport Container Loading to place packaged load units inside a container or trailer and review quantity, volume, payload, and remaining space.
- Use Corrugated Material & Strength for preliminary FEFCO 0201 material, pallet-load, compression, and carbon-screening estimates.
- Use Packaging Flow to pass selected, JSON-safe outputs through connected packaging stages without replacing the underlying tools.

## Public Expert boundary

The Public Expert explains approved public KolliPack knowledge. It does not receive form values, catalogue selections, current calculations, candidates, or results. It cannot calculate, change inputs, select candidates, apply changes, or operate a KolliPack tool.
