# Visual Editorial QA — Required before approval

This checklist **adds to**, but does not relax, the `blog-article/references/visual-editorial-critic.md` hard gate.

1. Compare new figure beside **both actual approved references**. Can a reader recognize same visual family?
2. View native width, ~900px article width, and ~360px mobile preview. Main idea must be clear; critical text readable.
3. Every central illustration shows the complete object: bottle base/cap, all RSC flaps, full pallet deck, etc. No cutoff subjects.
4. Check the relationship against the case. Stages, branches and connectors must represent the actual progression or engineering decision.
5. Check no invented values, fabricated competitor interface, copied benchmark from style reference, overconfident countable geometry, or edited screenshot metrics.
6. Overhang concepts must distinguish geometrically permitted overhang from approved transport safety; avoid a misleading green "shield" that certifies stability.
7. Takeaway is one evidence-supported sentence, not an absolute performance or safety claim.
8. Text must fit within the actual cards/banner; no overprint, low contrast or microtype.
9. Figure source role, caption, alt text and independent/reviewer status must be recorded.
10. User/owner approval remains separate. Prior approved figures never get overwritten silently.

Record a decision for visual fidelity and another for engineering meaning using the [Visual Editorial Critic](../../blog-article/references/visual-editorial-critic.md).

## Decision labels
`PASS` — self/independent visual QA passed, **not** owner publication approval.
`REVISE` — some composition/readability issues; correct at most one focused pass.
`FAIL` — unsupported factual claim, fake evidence, unreadable key values, or major composition defects.
`NOT REVIEWED` — final image cannot be visually inspected.

Treat generator/renderer success as proof only that a file rendered, **not** that the graphic is accurate, physically valid, or approved.
