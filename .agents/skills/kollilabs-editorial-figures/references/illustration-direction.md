# Subject-only Illustration Art Direction

Use both approved reference images under `../assets/` as the visual conditioning target if your generation tool supports true image references. For each visual subject, create *only the isolated physical scene* in a generous clear field. All graphic layouts, numbers and brand text are added later by the deterministic renderer.

## Reusable art brief
"Editorial three-quarter isometric product illustration consistent with the attached approved KolliLabs infographic artwork. Large realistic recognizable [SUBJECT] with natural cardboard/wood or believable product materials, soft neutral studio light, gentle contact shadow, clean almost-white background, subtle textures, exact complete silhouette with room around all sides. Calm friendly technical editorial style. No writing, numbers, labels, signage, brand marks, titles, badges, charts, arrows, photo-real promotional scenery, interface elements, or borders. No misleading visibly countable packing pattern. Centered object, generous margins."

## Specific objects
- **Bottle:** believable filled product bottle, rounded sidewall, neck and cap; nominal size is not represented by stretching the drawing.
- **Open RSC:** four credible flaps, visible walls; no extra flaps, floating caps or unsupported bottle count.
- **Pallet load:** whole deck and underside visible, upright cases, no extra floating layers or cut-off fork clearance.
- **Bag:** unmistakably a flexible bag, with closure appropriate to the supplied case.
- **Transport:** enclosure/opening clearly visible if the scenario actually contains that stage.
- **Concept:** use practical arrows/annotations later in layout where needed; avoid vague generic cubes or untestable "optimization" metaphors.

## Source/provenance
Record whether reference conditioning was really used; model/tool if available; subject prompt, output asset hash, and any postprocessing. A new generation is *not* a pixel-perfect reproduction of the same image. If exact consistency is needed, reuse approved subject artwork or a vetted product asset instead of regenerating it.
