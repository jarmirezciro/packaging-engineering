# Demo and Case-Study Article Profile

Use this profile when an article demonstrates a real KolliPack calculation or compares real tool alternatives.

## Purpose

A demo article should help a reader:

- Understand a practical packaging decision.
- See a verified KolliPack result.
- Understand the business consequence.
- Recognize what the calculation can and cannot decide.
- Open the exact prepopulated case and experiment with it.

## Mandatory inputs

- Exact case inputs.
- Author observations or `discussion.txt`.
- Current KolliPack backend access.
- Real KolliPack screenshots or approved figures.
- Relevant tool or workflow.
- Exact prepopulated case URL.

## Mandatory evidence process

1. Reproduce the supplied inputs through the current backend.
2. Compare backend outputs with screenshots, notes, and article claims.
3. Record useful alternatives and discrepancies.
4. Use only supported numerical claims.
5. Do not modify the engine during article work.

See `backend-evidence-policy.md`.

## Recommended narrative

Use this sequence as a story guide, not a rigid heading template:

1. Verified result-led opening.
2. Concrete business situation.
3. Initially attractive or obvious answer.
4. What KolliPack compared.
5. Main numerical or operational contrast.
6. Business consequence.
7. Remaining engineering, supplier, or operational decision.
8. Prepopulated case CTA.
9. Concise takeaway.

## Opening rule

Put the most important verified result within the first 100 words.

Weak:

> Packaging optimization involves several parameters.

Strong:

> The same twelve products produced two very different logistics outcomes: 9,600 products per container in one design and 14,400 in another.

## Business consequence

Translate the result into an understandable consequence, such as:

- Fewer boxes.
- More products per pallet.
- More products per container.
- Fewer illustrative shipments.
- Less estimated board area under stated assumptions.
- A narrower shortlist for physical testing.

Do not invent actual savings or operational performance.

## Calculation versus final decision

State clearly:

- What KolliPack calculated.
- Which alternatives appear promising.
- Which real-world factors remain.
- That physical tests, supplier drawings, material specifications, and operations may still decide the final design.

Say this once clearly. Do not weaken every result with repeated disclaimers.

## Prepopulated case requirement

Every demo article must include:

- Exact case URL in front matter CTA.
- Contextual body link or button.
- Article-specific footer CTA.
- New-tab behavior through the current blog implementation.
- A short explanation of what the reader can change.
- A warning when the case is missing or broken.

Do not replace a working case with a generic empty tool link.

## Images

A normal demo article should use approximately three to six purposeful figures.

Each figure should communicate one point:

- Setup.
- Main comparison.
- Downstream consequence.
- Detailed final result.
- Trade-off or shortlist.
- Final tool render.

Keep all relevant approved images, but combine related screenshots where that improves readability.

## Required review

Run:

- Backend evidence review.
- Editorial title and story review.
- Fresh-Eyes Business Reader review.
- SEO review.
- Minimal article validation.

## Publication state

Keep the article as `draft` until the author approves it.
