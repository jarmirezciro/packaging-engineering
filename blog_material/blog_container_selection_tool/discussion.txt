Purpose. Demo for Container Selection Tool or Box Calculation Tool. The idea is to show a case where a user that make calculations manually or without tolls would tend to make a decision, however with right tools can get a gain.

Case Inputs

product.
500x200x300
All restrictions allowed
Weight 2000g

container/box

800x800x900 

Packaging weight (g) 5000
Max payload (g) 50000

Business Background. Dimensions correspond to playwoodbox that can withstand 50000 kg, we whant to place a automotive sparepart for distribution around the world. After analysing position restriction, as most of the weight is supported by the plywoodbox we end up with the conclusion that all orientations are allowed and we use that as input for our tool


In the article we play with restrictions, first we go through each position restriction and then combine all restrictions and compare results, the idea is to show users how the tool works.

Observations

A person without right training, experience or simple right tools would choose the results that using only R3 delivers, which is 15, 78% efficiency.

However, with Kollipack allowing combining more than one position, you go to 94% efficiency.

We also discuss, but only for the maximised result, detail result as well, we also point out that we have optional and compulsory fields.

We can also mention that eventhough, this isolated case does not drive costs up, we can se that the accumulation of this kind of inneficiencies leads to it. Kollipack helps to standardize the way you work to avoid poor practices cost propagation.
