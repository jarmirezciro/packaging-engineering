Purpose: Show that designing packaging goes beyond a simple box. End to end thinking is necessary to get optimal results. Many parameters needs to be taken into account and therefore there is a need of automating calculations, the first "Nice" option is not necesarilly the most suitable one. We enphasize that the tool is only a aid, to prioritize options for testing.

Case: Company that manufactures a product xxxx dimensions 250x150x100, rectangular, please ChatGPT help to find an appropriate product. A huge supermarket chain requested to have the product packed in multiples of 12 in cardboard boxes, shipped in Europallets and Hight cube containers, the reason for this requirement is that manufacters ships product to supermarket chain central distribution center and from there to avoid extrahandling europallets are shipped to store network.


Packaging Flow Inputs

Product Dimensions 250x150x100, al orientations allowed, desired quantity 12, Design Mode
Pallet-Europallet, max height 1200 -Choose first option
High Cube Dry 40" Container, Max capacity.
